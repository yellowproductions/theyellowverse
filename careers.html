Senior Business Development

Mumbai · Full-time · On-site / Hybrid
Apply →

We're looking for a Senior Business Development resource to lead new business and grow The Yellowverse's client base across BFSI, industrial, real estate, retail and the Gulf. You'll own relationships end to end — from the first conversation to a signed engagement — and work closely with our strategy and creative teams to win the right work.

What you'll do

  • Identify and pursue new business across India and the GCC.
  • Own client relationships from first contact to signed brief.
  • Shape pitches and proposals alongside the creative team.
  • Represent The Yellowverse at industry events and build a referral network.
  • Own the pipeline, set growth targets, and shape the BD playbook as the team grows.

What we're looking for

  • 6+ years in business development or sales, with a track record of winning and owning senior client relationships — ideally at a branding, advertising, design or media agency.
  • A strong network and a real knack for opening doors.
  • Sharp communication; comfortable talking strategy with senior clients.
  • A self-starter who can work across India and the Gulf.
To apply, email creative@yellowproductions.co.in with the subject “Senior Business Development” and a short note on why you.

(How We Think)The Yellow

Most agencies spend three weeks understanding a brief. Then get it wrong.

The brief is never finished on paper. So we make early — not to be fast, to be right.

A brief only shows what it's missing once something real exists to react to. The work asks sharper questions than any meeting can.

And because the work is crafted, even an early draft earns trust instead of alarm. The loud idea fades. The well-made one survives the room.

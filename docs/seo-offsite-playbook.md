## 1. Google Business Profile — the single biggest local-ranking lever

You need **two separate profiles** (two real locations = two listings).

### A) Set up — Mumbai (Yellow Productions, Powai)
1. Go to **business.google.com** → *Add business* → enter the exact Name above.
2. **Category (most important field):**
   - Primary: `Advertising agency`
   - Additional: `Marketing agency`, `Graphic designer`, `Event management company`, `Video production service`
   - ⚠️ Do **not** stuff keywords into the *name* field (e.g. "Yellow Productions Creative Agency Mumbai"). It's a guideline violation and gets listings suspended. Keywords go in the **description**, not the name.
3. **Address:** exact string from §0. Choose to display it (you have a real office).
4. **Service area:** add Mumbai + MMR if you serve clients on-site.
5. **Phone / website:** §0 values. Website → the **/creative-agency-mumbai** page (not just the homepage).
6. **Description (750 chars, keyword-natural):**
   > The Yellowverse (Yellow Productions) is a creative & branding agency in Mumbai. From our Powai studio we take brands from strategy to on-ground execution — branding, campaigns, production, films, events, exhibitions and experiential — for BFSI, real estate, retail and industrial brands across India and the GCC. One accountable team, not seven vendors. Clients include Kotak, ICICI, Jindal, Vedanta and HDFC.
7. **Verification:** request the postcard/phone/video verification Google offers. Until verified, the profile won't rank.
8. **Photos (Google heavily rewards these):** logo, exterior/interior of the Powai office, team, and 15–20 work samples. Add a few every month.
9. **Services:** list each service (Branding, Campaigns, Event Management, Exhibition Stall Design, Social Media, Production) with a 1-line description each — these become keyword surfaces.

### B) Set up — Muscat (Yellow Designs & Services SPC)
Repeat the above with the Oman details. Get the **full Muscat street address first** (required). Categories same set. Description tuned for Oman/GCC + bilingual. Website → **/creative-agency-oman**.

### C) Ongoing (15 min/week, compounds over months)
- **Google Posts:** post 1×/week (a project, an essay link, an offer). Keeps the profile "active," which Google favours.
- **Reviews — the #1 ranking + conversion factor:** ask every happy client for a Google review. Target 10+ on each profile, then a steady trickle. Reply to every one. A simple ask: *"If you were happy with the work, a one-line Google review really helps us — here's the link."* Generate the short review link from the GBP dashboard.
- **Q&A:** seed 5–6 real FAQs (and answer them) on each profile.

## 3. Backlinks & Digital PR — what makes the new pages actually rank

New pages and essays only rank once other sites link to them. Tactics, ranked by impact-per-effort:

### Tier 1 — easy, do now
- **Agency directories (above)** — most give a do-follow link. Clutch, DesignRush, GoodFirms, Sortlist, The Manifest.
- **Client & partner links:** ask clients/partners whose brands you built to add "Brand & creative by The Yellowverse" with a link in their site credits / press. Vendors, fabricators, photographers you work with too.
- **Group cross-links:** make sure Yellow Productions, Perky Owl and Yellow Designs sites/profiles all link to theyellowverse.com (and vice-versa). (Already done in the site footer.)
- **Personal profiles:** founder LinkedIn + speaker bios → link to /manifesto.

### Tier 2 — medium effort, high authority
- **Awards:** enter Kyoorius, Indian Design Awards, ADC, Dubai Lynx / MENA awards, Abby Awards. Award pages link back and carry real authority + credibility.
- **Press / features:** pitch the Jindal stadium/league story and the GCC-branding angle to design & marketing press (Afaqs, Campaign India, Campaign Middle East, The Drum, Brand Equity). One good feature = one strong backlink + reputation.
- **Guest essays:** repurpose the /writing essays (strategy-vs-execution, stadium branding, GCC branding) as guest posts on industry blogs with a link back to the original.
- **Podcasts / panels:** founder appearances → show-notes backlinks.

### Tier 3 — ongoing flywheel
- **Keep publishing essays** (you now have 6). Each long-tail piece ("stadium branding beyond sponsorship logos", "build an image bank that lasts") is a link magnet and ranks for queries the big agencies ignore.
- **HARO / Featured / Qwoted:** answer journalist queries on branding/marketing → earns press links.
- **Case studies as link bait:** once the Jindal flagship case study is built with real numbers, pitch it as a story — transformation case studies attract links far better than portfolios.
